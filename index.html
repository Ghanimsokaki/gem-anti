Trust & safety

Download it. Verify it.

A trustworthy antivirus proves its installer is genuine. After downloading, compare your file's SHA-256 checksum with the official one:

In PowerShell run: Get-FileHash .\CyberGuard-Setup.exe — the hash should match exactly. If Windows SmartScreen appears on a brand-new app, click More info → Run anyway only if the checksum matches.
